Dementia Community Golf Cafe

Mill Green Golf Club is launching a new Dementia Community Cafe in partnership with Fore Get Me Not. The sessions will provide a safe, social and active space for people living with dementia to connect through the enjoyment of golf.

The community cafe will centre on socialising and refreshments, with light golf activities including putting, chipping, games and use of the driving range. For people who have played golf before, reconnecting with the sport can bring back happy memories. The session is also open to anyone living with dementia who may enjoy the social and active environment, whether or not they have played golf previously.

Tuesday 16 March 2027

The Hertfordshire Listening Event

A friendly and informal lived-experience listening event hosted by Alzheimer’s Society. It is an opportunity to share your experiences, tell us what matters to you and help shape Alzheimer’s Society’s understanding of what is important to people affected by dementia. The day will be welcoming and focused on listening to people’s experiences.

You do not need to have attended an Alzheimer’s Society event before, and you can share as much or as little as you feel comfortable with. We hope you can join us for the full day, but understand this may not be possible for everyone — you are welcome to leave at any point.

Who can attend?

This is a lived-experience listening event, rather than a professional consultation or networking event. It is open to anyone with lived experience of dementia, including:

  • People living with a diagnosis of dementia
  • People who are currently or have previously been caring for or supporting someone with dementia
  • People who are curious about dementia and would like to share their own thoughts, experiences or perspectives
  • Professionals who also have personal or lived experience of dementia, attending as someone sharing that lived experience
WhenTuesday 16 March 2027, 10:00am–2:00pm
WhereHoliday Inn Stevenage by IHG, Saint George’s Way, Stevenage, SG1 1HS
CostFree to attend. Lunch and drinks will be provided.
TravelTravel costs are reimbursed.
