7 October

Cyber and Fraud Aware — Hatfield Library

A free event with cyber and fraud crime prevention experts available to answer questions about staying safe and secure online, including passwords, two-step verification, phishing and social engineering, and phone, email and text scams. No tickets or booking required.

Wednesday 7 October, 11:00–14:15

11:00–12:00 — Scams & Victim Support – Beacon/Victim Care
12:00–13:00 — Rogue Traders & Scams – Trading Standards
13:00–13:15 — Fraud against the Council – SAFS
13:15–14:15 — Cyber Crime & Fraud Prevention – Herts Police

Hatfield Library. Further details: Hertfordshire Libraries or 0300 123 4049.

18 November — TBC

WHDFCP 10th Anniversary

We are planning to mark the 10th anniversary of the Welwyn Hatfield Dementia Friendly Community Partnership. Further details will be shared when confirmed.

7 December

Dementia-friendly Carol Service — St Etheldreda’s Church

Music24 welcomes everyone to a dementia-friendly Carol Service to celebrate Christmas together. After the service, guests are invited to join for light refreshments.

Monday 7 December 2026, 2:00–3:00pm

St Etheldreda’s Church, Fore Street, Old Hatfield, Hertfordshire, AL9 5AN.

Parking is available at Hatfield House via Church Lane. Please contact Dawn Knight on 07886 395299 or dawn@music24.org.uk for further information.

In partnership with St Etheldreda’s Church.

Stevenage Dementia Day

This event was rescheduled because of the very wet and thundery weather earlier in the summer. The festival is a free community event for people living with dementia, their families, carers, and the wider community. The afternoon will include music, entertainment, information stalls, and opportunities to connect with local organisations and support services.
