Upcoming events

Dates and details for events shared by the partnership and our local community.

12 September

Stevenage Dementia Day

This event was rescheduled because of the very wet and thundery weather earlier in the summer. The festival is a free community event for people living with dementia, their families, carers, and the wider community. The afternoon will include music, entertainment, information stalls, and opportunities to connect with local organisations and support services.

21 September

World Alzheimer’s Day

A day to raise awareness of Alzheimer’s disease and dementia and to support people affected by dementia around the world.

18 November — TBC

WHDFCP 10th Anniversary

We are planning to mark the 10th anniversary of the Welwyn Hatfield Dementia Friendly Community Partnership. Further details will be shared when confirmed.
