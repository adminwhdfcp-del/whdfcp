Welwyn Grange Care Home (Ardale)

Welwyn Grange is a Hertfordshire family run Residential Home with Nursing, which challenges most people's perceptions of life in a care home. It is made up of small Households which enables likeminded peers of no more than 16 people to live well together.

For those living with a memory problem or a Dementia, their bespoke environment uses dementia friendly design best practice such as colour coding, clear signage, lighting and memory boxes, so the lived experience and scale is truly home from home, empowering all to live a life meaningful to them, with as little stress as possible.

Welwyn Hatfield Borough Council

Welwyn Hatfield Borough Council supports people living with dementia by working in partnership with local organisations, health services, community groups, and volunteers. Together, we provide opportunities for people living with dementia and their carers to stay connected, active, and supported through inclusive activities, wellbeing programmes, and community spaces.

Through our Healthy Hub, we are able to signpost carers to local support services. We also support and promote national campaigns that raise awareness and understanding of dementia across the borough.

As part of our ongoing commitment, we are currently working towards dementia-friendly accreditation and will be encouraging businesses and community organisations to play an active role in making Welwyn Hatfield more dementia friendly.

Dementia Friendly & Memory Support Hertfordshire

Memory Support Hertfordshire is a countywide service for people who may or may not have a formal diagnosis of dementia, as well as for their families and carers. The service provides a single point of access through one telephone number and is delivered in partnership by Age UK Hertfordshire, Alzheimer’s Society, and Carers in Hertfordshire.

Age UK Hertfordshire manages the single point of access - providing practical support, information, advice, wellbeing services, and opportunities to stay connected within the community.

Alzheimer’s Society provides support through Dementia Advisors, group support for the person living with dementia and their carers and young onset support.

Carers in Hertfordshire provides support, advice, advocacy, and resources for unpaid carers, managed and delivered through their Learning and Development Officer.

My role focuses on Dementia Friendly Communities, supporting organisations to become more dementia-inclusive by raising awareness of dementia and encouraging them to work towards dementia-friendly accreditation. This involves helping organisations identify practical improvements that can enhance the experience of people living with dementia.

We are also currently promoting a small grants programme for customer-facing organisations. The funding can be used to improve the physical accessibility of their premises, helping people living with dementia to navigate spaces more easily.
