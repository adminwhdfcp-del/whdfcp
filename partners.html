Our partners

Find local organisations, services, businesses and community groups working with the Welwyn Hatfield Dementia Friendly Community Partnership across Welwyn Hatfield, Hertfordshire.

The organisations below are part of the local partnership network. They each contribute in different ways to making Welwyn Hatfield a more dementia-friendly community.

Hertfordshire Hearing Advisory Services

Hertfordshire Hearing Advisory Service supports NHS Audiology across Hertfordshire, Bedfordshire and Suffolk, improving wellbeing for people with hearing loss. The charity ensures hearing aids are well maintained and provides clinics, care home visits, deaf awareness sessions and hearing aider training, delivered by skilled staff and volunteers to strengthen communication and daily life.

Hatfield Library

Hatfield Library is a dementia‑friendly, fully accessible space with trained staff, ground‑floor facilities, quiet areas and helpful tools like a visual story, hearing loop and communication aids. It offers warm or cool refuge, weekly social groups and a welcoming environment for people living with dementia and their carers.

WGC Library

Hatfield and Welwyn Garden City Libraries provide dementia‑friendly support, offering information, signposting and trained staff working toward accreditation. Both libraries partner with the Welwyn Hatfield Dementia Friendly Community Partnership Group to offer accessible spaces, helpful resources and welcoming assistance for people living with dementia and their carers.

Welwyn Grange Care Home (Ardale)

Welwyn Grange is a family‑run residential home with nursing, designed around small households that help residents live well together. Its dementia‑friendly environment uses colour coding, clear signage, lighting and memory boxes to reduce stress and support people with dementia, creating a true home‑from‑home where meaningful, independent living is encouraged.

Right At Home Welwyn, Hatfield & St Albans

HCC Trading Standards

Hertfordshire Trading Standards protects vulnerable residents from fraud, especially those living with dementia. They install free landline call‑blocking devices to stop criminal calls and give carers peace of mind when away. The team also delivers talks to local carer groups, highlighting current scams and practical ways to stay safe.

Welwyn Hatfield Borough Council

They offer inclusive activities, wellbeing programmes and signposting via the Healthy Hub, promote national dementia‑awareness campaigns, and are working toward dementia‑friendly accreditation while encouraging local businesses and organisations to help make the borough more dementia friendly.
