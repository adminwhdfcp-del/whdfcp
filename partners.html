Hatfield Library

Hatfield Library is a valuable hub for people living with dementia and their carers, for information and signposting to key resources. Friendly library staff are trained in dementia awareness, working to achieve accreditation. Our library is dementia friendly, and we work with other partners locally through the Welwyn Hatfield Dementia Friendly Community Partnership Group, providing help and support. We are once again proud to support Dementia Action Week, and this year we will be holding staff led Memory Walks to and from The Bunker Museum, Hatfield on the 19th May, followed by refreshments in the library and where attendees will be able to view our wonderful historical local maps, beautiful historical photographs from decades past, and artifacts etc. (Booking in the library is essential).

Trading Standards, Hertfordshire County Council

Hertfordshire CC Trading Standards tries to protect and support vulnerable residents who are being targeted by fraudsters. For those who are living with dementia, we can install call blocking devices for landlines free of charge, to stop criminals targeting the most vulnerable. It allows carers to leave the home without worrying about their loved one answering the phone. We also give talks to local groups of carers, to warn them about current scams and how to keep safe.
